CLEAR INFORMATION, NO SURPRISES

Privacy.

SafeWalk is a browser-only demo. It has no application server or user accounts.

What SafeWalk stores

SafeWalk does not send your saved trip, trusted contact, recent searches, safety checklist, or onboarding preference to a SafeWalk server. These items may be stored in this browser’s local storage:

  • Planned trip place names, search text, distance, walking-time estimate, and turn-by-turn directions. Exact coordinates from a current-location route are not saved.
  • Recent places you searched for.
  • A trusted contact name and phone number, if you save one.
  • Your checked walking safety checklist items, if you use the checklist.
  • Whether you dismissed the first-visit explanation.

Browser storage is not encrypted by SafeWalk. Other people with access to this device or browser profile may be able to view it.

When location is used

SafeWalk asks the browser for location only after you press a current-location, one-time location-share, or alert-share button. It does not request location on page load or track location in the background.

If you choose to share, the browser opens a share sheet or copy prompt. You choose the recipient and whether to send. SafeWalk does not deliver messages or create a live tracking link.

External map and sharing services

When you request directions, the entered place names are sent to Photon for search and the resulting route coordinates are sent to the public OpenStreetMap-based routing service. If you press the current-location button, your coordinates are also sent to Photon to look up a place name. If precise location is unavailable and you agree to an approximate, city-level location, your IP address is sent to ipapi.co. Map tiles come from OpenStreetMap. These providers may receive normal network information such as your IP address under their own policies.

Sharing uses your browser or operating system’s share and dialer features. Their behavior and privacy policies are outside SafeWalk’s control.

Clear SafeWalk data

This removes SafeWalk’s trip, recent-place, trusted-contact, safety-checklist, and onboarding entries from this browser. It does not clear browser history, shared messages, service-worker files, or data held by third-party services.

Demo limitations

SafeWalk does not provide safety ratings or safety-zone data. Route and travel-time estimates are not safety reports; check local conditions and trusted official sources before your walk. There is no live tracking, automatic SOS, guardian service, or emergency dispatch. In India, the emergency number links are 112, Women Helpline 1091, and Women Helpline 181; a dial link opens your device’s phone app.
